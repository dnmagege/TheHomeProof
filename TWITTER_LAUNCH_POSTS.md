## Engagement Rules (Critical for Twitter)

### Reply to Landlord Mentions
Watch for tweets about:
- "Lost deposit dispute"
- "Rental property frustration"
- "Tenant issues"

**Reply Strategy:**
```
Original: "Just lost £500 on a deposit claim. No evidence. So frustrated."

Your Reply:
"That sucks, and it's way too common. What if there was a way to prevent this happening again?

We just built TheHomeProof - AI that organizes property evidence automatically.

Free to try: [link]

Genuinely sorry that happened 😞"
```

### Daily Tweet Ideas (Easy)
- Monday: Tip on organizing property photos
- Tuesday: Tenancy law question + answer
- Wednesday: Before/after evidence example
- Thursday: User success story (once you have them)
- Friday: Week recap + call to try
- Weekend: Casual take on landlord/tenant issues

## Engagement Checklist (Daily)
- [ ] Reply to all mentions within 2 hours
- [ ] Reply to comments on your posts
- [ ] Find 3-5 relevant tweets to engage with (reply thoughtfully)
- [ ] Tweet once (or schedule)
- [ ] Check follower count (watch growth)

**Time investment: 15-20 min/day**

---

## Success Metrics for Twitter

**Week 1:**
- 50-100 followers
- 20-50 engagement per tweet
- 1-2 clicks to app per tweet

**Week 2-3:**
- 200+ followers (if you engage well)
- 100+ engagement per tweet
- 5-10 signups from Twitter

**Typical conversion:**
- 1 viral tweet (500+ likes) = 10-20 signups
- 10 regular tweets = 2-5 signups

**Goal: Get 1 tweet to 200+ engagements in first month**

---

## If Someone Replies Negatively

**Bad Reply:**
```
"Actually, this saves time because..."
```

**Good Reply:**
```
"Fair point. What would make it actually useful for you?"
```

**Best Reply:**
```
"You're right. We're still learning what people need. 
Would love to understand your frustration better. 
Can we DM?"
```

People who criticize often become your best customers if you listen.

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Use Images** - Tweet screenshots of your AI in action
2. **Ask Questions** - "What's your biggest landlord frustration?" gets replies
3. **Share Wins** - "100 signups!" keeps momentum going
4. **Be Honest** - "We're in beta and learning" builds trust
5. **Engage First** - Reply & retweet others before promoting
