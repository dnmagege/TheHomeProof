# TheHomeProof - Executive Summary & Launch Brief

**For**: Founder | **Date**: June 2026 | **Status**: Ready to Deploy

---

## 🎯 In One Sentence

**TheHomeProof** is an AI-powered platform that turns rental property photos, contracts, and inspections into tribunal-ready evidence in minutes—solving the UK's deposit dispute crisis and protecting both landlords and tenants.

---

## 📊 The Business Opportunity

### Problem Size
- **2.7M** rental properties in UK
- **~30%** of deposit disputes end up in tribunal
- **£1B+** estimated in disputed deposits annually
- **5+ hours** of manual work per property per year
- **No market leader** - OpenRent focuses on listings, not evidence

### Our Solution
**One platform. Complete evidence lifecycle.**

| Task | Manual Time | TheHomeProof | Savings |
|------|------------|--------------|---------|
| Generate inventory | 2 hours | 10 minutes | 91% faster |
| Analyze contract | 1 hour | 2 minutes | 97% faster |
| Compare damage | 1.5 hours | 5 minutes | 94% faster |
| Build dispute evidence | 4 hours | 30 minutes | 87% faster |

### Target Customer
**Landlords with 1-10 properties who:**
- Manage properties independently (not using agents)
- Have experienced deposit disputes
- Care about compliance
- Want to save time & protect evidence

**Also**: Tenants who want to protect their deposits

## ✅ What's Built & Working

### Core Features (100% Complete)
- ✅ AI Inventory Generator (photos → structured room inventory)
- ✅ AI Contract Parser (PDF/text → extracted terms & obligations)
- ✅ AI Damage Detection (before/after photos → damage report & deductions)
- ✅ AI Rent Estimator (property details → market rent with confidence)
- ✅ AI Co-Pilot Chat (24/7 UK tenancy law assistant)
- ✅ Dispute Evidence Builder (compile all data into tribunal-ready bundle)
- ✅ Stripe Subscriptions (Free/Pro/Business with feature gates)
- ✅ Multi-language (8 languages) + Multi-currency (19 currencies)
- ✅ Mobile-responsive UI + Dark mode
- ✅ Secure Supabase backend with RLS policies
- ✅ Email via Resend (tenant invites, notifications)
- ✅ Rate limiting + API authentication

### Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: Next.js 14, React 18, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui
- **Backend**: Next.js API routes (dynamic routing)
- **Database**: Supabase PostgreSQL with RLS
- **AI**: OpenAI GPT-4o
- **Payments**: Stripe
- **Hosting**: Vercel (auto-deploy from GitHub)
- **Email**: Resend
- **Monitoring**: Sentry (error tracking)
